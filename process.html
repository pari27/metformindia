Process
  
Cold forging is a process that re-shapes metal at room temperature by the application of a force.
The grain structure of the material undergoes deformation, producing a component much stronger in comparison to a similar machined component.
It has the advantage of less material wastage and more precise tolerances.
It is an extremely consistent and cost effective manufacturing solution.
