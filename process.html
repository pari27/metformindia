Process

Cold forging

Cold forging is a process that re-shapes metal at room temperature by the application of a force.

  1. 01The grain structure of the material undergoes deformation, producing a component much stronger in comparison to a similar machined component.
  2. 02It has the advantage of less material wastage and more precise tolerances.
  3. 03It is an extremely consistent and cost effective manufacturing solution.

Metal Forming by cold forging. Supplier to the Automotive, Electrical and Aerospace Industries.
