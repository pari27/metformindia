About us

METFORM ENGINEERS was a pioneer in establishing the Cold Forging metal forming process and we pride ourselves on being one of the leading suppliers of components to the Automotive Industry today. We process more than 100 tons of Steel every month to deliver 2 million components and our total turnover for the year 2010 was over Rs.5 crores. We understand the needs of today’s customer and therefore the guiding principle behind everything we make is, and will remain, quality.

OUR FOUNDER

The Company was founded by Mr. VELLORE P RANGA (M.Tech, IIT) in 1980. He continues to manage it to this very day and is ably assisted by the next generation. The unit has established a name for its quality and is very well established among the Leading Automobile and Engineering Industries in India and abroad.

OUR PLANT

The Company is located on a 4850 sq. meter plant in Jigani Industrial Area, Bangalore and currently has more than 100 employees. We now have 13 Cold Forging Presses ranging from 100 to 800 ton capacity as well as a bank of Automats, Thread-rolling and CNC machines.

WHAT WE MAKE

We currently specialize in the manufacture of cold-forged Steel, Copper and Aluminium components which are used in the Automotive, Electrical and Aerospace Industry.

Metal Forming by cold forging. Supplier to the Automotive, Electrical and Aerospace Industries.
